# hey, I'm Garry (or notgarryy) 👋

Electrical Engineering grad who spent most of uni poking at neural networks and radar signals. I like building things that sit at the intersection of ML and embedded systems — sometimes they even work.

---

### a few things I've worked on

- **Fall Detection System** — FMCW radar + deep learning (TCN-based) to detect falls. Ended up published in [IEEE Access](https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/) (Q1). Probably the project I'm most proud of, even if radar signal processing nearly broke me.
- **TTSNet** — another research project, published in a SINTA 1 journal. Speech synthesis direction.
- **Fall Detection API** — took the research and wrapped it into something deployable: FastAPI, Docker, MLflow, Railway, GitHub Actions. Trying to close the gap between "model that works in a notebook" and "model that works in the real world".
- **Portfolio site** — [notgarryy-portofolio.vercel.app](https://notgarryy-portofolio.vercel.app/)
